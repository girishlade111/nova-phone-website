Our Story

About NOVA

We believe technology should empower, not overwhelm. That is why we build smartphones that adapt to you — not the other way around.

Our Mission

Technology That Disappears

Founded in 2019, NOVA Technologies was born from a simple frustration: smartphones had become too complicated. Too many settings. Too many compromises. Too much noise.

We set out to build something different — a phone that understands you. One that anticipates your needs, protects your privacy, and gets out of your way so you can focus on what matters.

Today, NOVA serves over 50 million users across 120 countries. But our mission remains the same: to make technology feel invisible, intuitive, and truly yours.

Our Values

What We Stand For

Simplicity First

We remove complexity, not features. Every interaction should feel effortless and intuitive.

Privacy by Design

Your data belongs to you. We build with on-device AI and end-to-end encryption at the core.

Sustainability

Carbon-neutral manufacturing, 5-year software support, and 95% recyclable packaging.

Open Platform

We believe in interoperability. NOVA devices work seamlessly with your existing ecosystem.
